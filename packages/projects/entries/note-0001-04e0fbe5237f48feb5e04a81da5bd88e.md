## Level 5 — Projects: Make Individual Learning Organizational

**The constraint this solves:** good practices remain local. Once playbooks exist, the next question isn't about individual productivity anymore — it's how an organization connects multiple bodies of knowledge, teams, and agents so that each project makes the *organization* smarter, not just the person or team who did the work.

**Cartenza's response:** Projects, composing multiple knowledge packages, routines, and playbooks around a larger objective. The unit of value shifts from "a better AI session" to a durable organizational asset — captured experience that can be reused, executed, refined, and scaled across more than one team or effort.

**Status as of this writing:** this is the least built-out level — a real design thread (`cousin_back_log`/note-0049), not yet a concrete, working instance the way the log book is. If you're exploring this level, you're at the frontier of what's actually designed, not reading about something already proven in use.

**A concrete signal you'd be reaching for this level:** you're trying to connect knowledge/routines/playbooks that currently live in separate, unrelated packages or repos, because they're actually part of one larger effort — and the current tools don't yet give you a clean way to say that.
